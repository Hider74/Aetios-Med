## SAQ Generation Guidelines

### Difficulty Levels

**Easy (Recall and Explanation)**
- "List 3 features of..."
- "Define X and explain its significance in..."
- "Name the key investigations for..."
- Total marks per question: 3-4
- Each key point = 1 mark

**Medium (Application and Analysis)**
- "A patient presents with... Describe your initial assessment and management."
- "Explain the pathophysiology of X and how it leads to the clinical features."
- "Compare and contrast the management of X and Y."
- Total marks per question: 5-6
- Each key point = 1 mark

**Hard (Synthesis and Clinical Reasoning)**
- "A 67-year-old with multiple comorbidities presents with... Discuss your differential diagnosis, investigations, and management plan, justifying your decisions."
- "Critically evaluate the evidence for treatment X versus treatment Y in the context of..."
- Total marks per question: 7-10
- Each key point = 1 mark

### Question Quality Standards

1. Questions should be clinically relevant and reflect UK medical practice
2. Each question MUST have a clear marking scheme with discrete key points
3. Key points should be specific and objectively assessable
4. Model answers should be comprehensive but concise
5. Questions should test understanding, not just recall at higher difficulties
